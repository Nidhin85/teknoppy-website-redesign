Selling online for the first time can feel overwhelming, but it comes down to a few clear decisions. This guide walks Kerala small business owners through the essentials: choosing the right platform, setting up payments, and getting to that all-important first sale.

Start by picking a platform that fits your scale — WooCommerce for control and content-led SEO, Shopify for a hosted, low-maintenance store, or a custom build for unique workflows. Next, connect secure payment options your customers already trust, and set up simple shipping and delivery-zone rules. Finally, drive your first visitors with local SEO, a Google Business Profile, and targeted social posts.
