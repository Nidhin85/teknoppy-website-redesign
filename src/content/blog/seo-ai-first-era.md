Search is changing faster than at any point in the last decade. AI answer engines and generative search results — from Google's AI Overviews to assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity — are reshaping how people find and choose businesses. Ranking on page one still matters, but it is no longer the whole story.

Forward-thinking SEO now optimises for answer engines and generative search (AEO and GEO) as well as traditional rankings. That means clear, question-led content, verifiable facts AI can cite, structured schema markup, and strong E-E-A-T signals — real client results, named authorship, and genuine reviews. Businesses that adapt early will be the ones AI assistants recommend.
