02 · Background

About Dawar Qatar

Dawar Global Trading and Contracting supplies 12 product lines to oil and gas, construction and marine projects across Qatar. Its buyers start on Google with very specific searches: a product, a part number, a standard or a location.

Dawar is a Doha trading and contracting company that sources and delivers oilfield equipment, MRO supplies, welding consumables, marine supplies, electrical items, PPE, lifting equipment, hydraulic and industrial tools, valves and fittings, hospitality supplies and NDT equipment. It also offers procurement, project support and logistics, and promises quotes within 24 hours.

03 · The challenge

Thousands of specific searches in a crowded market

Three problems shaped the SEO strategy.

01

A crowded head keyword

"Industrial supplies in Qatar" is contested by large trading houses and business directories. A new site needed more than one keyword to win traffic.

02

Thousands of very specific searches

Buyers search for exact items like E6013 or E7018 electrodes, chain block hoists, hydraulic torque wrenches and explosion-proof cable glands. Each needs a page that clearly answers that search.

03

Local and technical intent together

Searches combine product, standard and place, such as "ATEX cable gland supplier Ras Laffan". The site had to show Google where Dawar delivers and which standards it meets.

05 · Our approach

How Teknoppy did the SEO

5 connected pillars, each answering a problem from the audit.

Pillar 01 · Research

Keyword research and mapping

We grouped keywords into four layers and gave each layer its own pages, so no two pages compete for the same search.

Pillar 02 · On-page

On-page SEO

The homepage title reads "Industrial Supplies in Qatar | Oil & Gas, PPE, Tools | Dawar" with a matching H1 and meta description. Every section uses a clean H2 and H3 structure, images carry descriptive alt text, and canonical and Open Graph tags are set so pages share and index correctly.

Pillar 03 · Content

Content for answer engines

An 8-question FAQ answers what buyers ask before an RFQ. Three buying guides target informational searches: lifting equipment colour codes, E6013 vs E7018 electrodes, and how to write an RFQ.

Pillar 04 · Local

Local SEO for Qatar

The full Doha address, phone, WhatsApp and working hours appear in the footer for consistent name, address and phone details. A delivery coverage section names Doha, the Industrial Area, Ras Laffan, Mesaieed and Dukhan, matching the places buyers search.

Pillar 05 · Linking

Internal linking and trust signals

Products link to industries, services and brands, so Google understands how the catalogue fits together. Standards such as API, ASME, ATEX and IECEx, named brands and recent projects add the expertise and trust signals Google looks for in industrial content.

10 · Direct answers

Frequently asked questions

How long does SEO take to show results in Qatar?

Most B2B sites start to see ranking movement in 3 to 6 months. Competitive head keywords take longer, while long-tail product and location searches often move first.

What is local SEO for a B2B company in Qatar?

It means showing Google exactly where you are and where you deliver: a consistent address and phone number, a Google Business Profile, and pages that name the areas you serve, such as Doha, Ras Laffan and Mesaieed.

Can product part numbers rank on Google?

Yes. Buyers often search exact codes like E7018. Pages that name the part, its standard and its use clearly can rank well for these low-competition, high-intent searches.

Do FAQs and guides help SEO?

Yes. They answer the questions buyers ask, can win featured snippets, and give AI tools such as Google AI Overviews short, clear answers to quote.

Does Teknoppy offer SEO services in Qatar?

Yes. Teknoppy is an SEO and web design company in Kochi, Kerala, running SEO for businesses in Qatar, the UAE and across the GCC.

What does an SEO audit for an industrial website cover?

Keywords, page titles and headings, site speed and Core Web Vitals, indexing, internal links, mobile usability, local SEO and content gaps against competitors.
