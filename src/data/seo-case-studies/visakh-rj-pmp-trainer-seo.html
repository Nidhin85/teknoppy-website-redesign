02 · Background

About Visakh RJ

Visakh RJ is a Mumbai-based PMP® trainer and leadership coach, and an alumnus of NIT Calicut. His site states 15+ years of global project experience, 10,000+ professionals mentored and a 99% pass rate, and highlights his recognition as "The PMP Production Machine" by Lee R. Lambert and an India Book of Records entry for the most PMP students cleared in a year. He teaches predictive, agile and hybrid project management, runs the teAchnology YouTube channel, and publishes a blog of more than 100 posts focused on the 2026 PMP exam changes, PMBOK updates and the PMP application process.

As a personal brand, he competes for searches like "PMP trainer India" against large training platforms such as StarAgile, ProThoughts, Henry Harvin and Simpliaxis. The site had real proof and deep content, but its structured data did not describe him or his course, its meta description was too long to display, and its keyword coverage stopped at Mumbai.

This case study covers the schema, keyword research, meta optimization and backlink content delivered by Teknoppy. It does not cover Visakh RJ's training curriculum or exam results, which are his own domain.

03 · The challenge

Helping one trainer compete with national training platforms

Our first audit found five problems holding the site back.

01

A person competing with platforms

Large training companies dominate PMP searches with hundreds of pages. A single trainer needs search engines to recognise him as a distinct, credible expert, not just another training listing.

02

No Course schema

The PMP training programme — the core offer — had no Course markup at all, so the site was missing the structured data most directly tied to what buyers search for.

03

Meta tags too long to display

The meta description ran to 234 characters, so search results cut it off before the strongest proof points could appear.

04

Keyword coverage stopped at Mumbai

The site only mentioned Mumbai, so searches for PMP training in Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Kochi or Delhi NCR had no page to land on — and there was no course or pricing page either.

05

Few links pointing to the brand

A personal site has fewer natural backlinks than a training company. It needed a steady, natural-looking link profile from directories and editorial sites, without over-repeating one exact-match anchor.

05 · Our approach

How we built personal-brand SEO for a PMP trainer

Six pillars took the site from strong content with weak signals to a clearly defined expert entity.

Pillar 01 · Entity

A Person entity at the centre

Teknoppy built the schema around a Person block for Visakh RJ — job title, Mumbai location, NIT Calicut, areas of expertise from PMP to agile and hybrid project management, awards, languages and his LinkedIn, YouTube and Instagram profiles. WebSite, WebPage and Organization blocks reference it by @id, so every page points back to one clearly defined expert.

Pillar 02 · Course schema

Markup for the core offer

Course schema was added for the PMP certification training — the highest-value structured data for the site and previously missing — describing the programme, its predictive, agile and hybrid coverage, and the three exam domains of People, Process and Business Environment.

Pillar 03 · Meta

Third-party proof first

The meta description was rewritten from 234 to 158 characters and now opens with the Lee R. Lambert recognition, followed by pass rate, experience and professionals mentored. Leading with what someone else said, rather than a self-claim, is the signal AI answer engines weigh most when asked who the best PMP trainer is.

Pillar 04 · Research

132 keywords from 101 blog posts

With Semrush data unavailable, Teknoppy went through the site by hand — homepage, About page and every blog post — and mapped 132 keywords into a spreadsheet. Section A covers terms the site already targets, from brand searches and "PMP trainer India" to blog topics like PMP exam changes 2026, PMBOK 8th edition and PMP application steps.

Pillar 05 · Gaps

City and course opportunities

Section B lists keywords with a high chance to rank but no page yet: PMP training in Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Kochi, Kerala and Delhi NCR, Navi Mumbai coaching, classroom training in Mumbai and a PMP bootcamp in India — plus course and pricing keywords that need a dedicated course page.

Pillar 06 · Backlinks

A natural backlink content kit

Teknoppy wrote paragraphs for directory and guest submissions around "PMP certification trainer India", in linked and plain-text versions, and fully rewritten for each site so no two submissions are identical. The kit mixes in branded anchors like "Visakh RJ", URL anchors like "rjvisakh.com", and long-tail and location anchors to keep the profile natural.

09 · Strategic reasoning

Why this approach matters

A personal brand cannot out-publish a national training platform page for page. What it can do is be unmistakably defined: one person, one course, one set of credentials, described the same way everywhere. Person and Course schema, connected by shared IDs, turns a name into an entity that search engines understand — and that is what lets one trainer stand next to companies in the results.

AI answer engines raise the stakes. When someone asks ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews who the best PMP trainer in India is, those tools lean on third-party proof and consistent facts rather than self-description. Putting an endorsement and a records entry first, keeping details consistent, and building a natural spread of links gives them good reason to name Visakh RJ in the answer.

Defining Visakh RJ as one clear expert entity — with his course, his credentials and his third-party proof connected in structured data — is what lets a single trainer compete with national platforms in both Google and AI search.

10 · Due diligence FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What was the main SEO challenge for Visakh RJ?

Visakh RJ is a person, not a company, competing for PMP training searches against large training platforms. The site needed search engines and AI tools to recognise him as a distinct expert, but it had no Course schema, no course or pricing page, and keyword coverage limited mainly to Mumbai.

What schema markup did Teknoppy build?

Teknoppy built a connected schema graph centred on a Person entity for Visakh RJ, with his job title, location, alma mater, awards, areas of expertise and social profiles, plus WebSite, WebPage and Organization blocks. It also added Course schema for the PMP certification training, which had been missing entirely.

How many keywords were researched?

Teknoppy reviewed the homepage, About page and all 101 blog posts and mapped 132 keywords. They were split into keywords the site already targets — brand, core training and blog topics such as the 2026 PMP exam changes — and keywords with a high chance to rank, such as city and course pages.

What gaps did the keyword research find?

The site only mentioned Mumbai, so city searches such as PMP training in Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Kochi and Delhi NCR had no landing pages. There was also no dedicated course or pricing page, a major gap for buyers comparing PMP training options.

What was in the backlink content kit?

The kit includes paragraphs built around the anchor "PMP certification trainer India", plain-text versions for sites that do not allow links, and a fresh rewrite for each submission. It recommends mixing branded anchors like "Visakh RJ" and URL anchors like "rjvisakh.com" so the link profile looks natural.

Why is third-party proof important for a personal brand?

AI answer engines and Google trust endorsements from others more than self-claims. Visakh RJ's recognition as "The PMP Production Machine" by Lee R. Lambert and his India Book of Records entry were placed in the meta description and schema so they are the first things search engines read.

How was the meta description written?

The original description ran to 234 characters and would be cut off in search results. Teknoppy rewrote it to 158 characters, leading with the Lee R. Lambert endorsement and keeping the pass rate, experience and professionals-mentored figures.

Does this approach work for other coaches and trainers?

Yes. Any trainer, coach or consultant whose business is their name benefits from Person and Course schema, third-party proof placed up front, city and course landing pages, and a natural, varied backlink profile.
