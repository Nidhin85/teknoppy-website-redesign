02 · Background

About Liberty Car Care

Liberty Car Care is a tyre, battery and car service business in Abu Dhabi, serving drivers since 2009. It sells and fits tyres and batteries, and offers wheel alignment and balancing, puncture repair, oil changes, vehicle inspection, brake work and general repairs.

The business runs seven service centres across the city: Mussafah M-10, Muroor, Al Mushrif, Al Manasir, Motor World in Shamkha, Al Falah and Riyadh City. Several branches sit inside ADNOC petrol stations, where drivers already stop.

Customers can also order tyres online and have them delivered and fitted at their home or office the same day, and pay after the job is done. Teknoppy was brought in to make this offer visible to every driver searching in Abu Dhabi.

03 · The challenge

Making seven branches look like seven strong local answers

Our first audit found five problems holding the site back.

01

Seven branches, one weak local signal

Branch names, addresses and phone numbers varied across maps and directories, so Google could not confidently match searches to the nearest branch.

02

A name shared with other businesses

"Liberty Car Care" is also the name of unrelated workshops in the United States, which can confuse search engines and AI tools.

03

More than one website and many listings

Older domains and directory profiles described the business in different ways, splitting authority.

04

Services competing for the same searches

Tyre, battery and car service content overlapped, so no single page clearly owned searches like "tyre shop in Mussafah".

05

Two languages, one market

Many Abu Dhabi drivers search in Arabic. English and Arabic content needed to target matching searches without competing.

“
Key takeaway

A business with seven branches should look like seven strong local answers, not one blurred listing.

05 · Our approach

How we built multi-location local SEO in Abu Dhabi

Eight steps turned scattered listings into one consistent, bilingual local presence.

Pillar 01 · Research

Search-intent research by service and area

We studied how drivers actually search: by service ("wheel alignment"), urgency ("same-day tyre fitting"), area ("Mussafah", "Al Mushrif") and language.

Pillar 02 · Mapping

One service, one page

Tyres, batteries, wheel alignment, puncture repair, oil change and inspection each received a dedicated target. Overlapping copy was rewritten so pages support each other instead of competing.

Pillar 03 · Local

Branch-level local SEO

We aligned name, address, phone and opening hours for each branch across Google Business Profile, maps and UAE directories, and linked every listing to the main website.

Pillar 04 · Entity

Entity disambiguation

We made "Liberty Car Care, Abu Dhabi, UAE" explicit in titles, About content and Organization and AutoRepair schema, so search engines do not mix it up with unrelated businesses.

Pillar 05 · Bilingual

Bilingual on-page SEO

English and Arabic pages were optimised as matched pairs with their own titles and descriptions, so each language ranks for its own searches.

Pillar 06 · Mobile

Content for urgent, mobile searches

Most car service searches happen on a phone, often at the roadside. Key pages show the call and WhatsApp buttons first, with same-day fitting and pay-after-service made clear.

Pillar 07 · AEO

Answer-first content

Key pages open with a short, direct answer: what the service is, where it is available, how fast and what it costs to book.

Pillar 08 · Seasonal

Content for UAE driving conditions

Abu Dhabi's summer heat is hard on tyres and batteries. We planned seasonal content on tyre pressure, heat damage and battery checks, so Liberty appears when drivers search before and during summer.

Key takeaway

10 · Strategic reasoning

Why consistency wins local car service searches

For a car service business, most searches are local and urgent. A driver with a flat tyre in Al Mushrif wants the nearest trusted branch now, not a list of every workshop in the UAE. Clear service pages, consistent branch details and bilingual content let Google, Google Maps and AI assistants send that driver straight to Liberty Car Care.

Abu Dhabi drivers also search in two languages and across many neighbourhoods. A business that answers clearly in both English and Arabic, for each area it serves, earns more of those searches than a larger competitor with vague pages.

Removing confusion with similarly named businesses also protects the brand. When someone asks an AI tool about "Liberty Car Care", the answer should describe the Abu Dhabi tyre and service centre, its branches and its same-day fitting. The same structure makes growth easier too: a new branch or service gets its own clear page and listing, on a site search engines already understand.

Seven branches, one consistent identity, and a clear page for every service in both languages: that is what lets Google Maps and AI assistants send a stranded driver to the nearest Liberty Car Care.
