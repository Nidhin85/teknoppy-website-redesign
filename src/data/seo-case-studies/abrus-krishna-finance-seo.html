03 · About

About Abrus Krishna Finance

Abrus Krishna Finance is a lending company in Guruvayoor, Kerala, registered under the Kerala Money-Lenders Act, 1958 (KML). It has worked in the financial sector since 2018 and serves individuals, families and small businesses in Guruvayoor and the wider region.

Its eight loan products cover gold loans, doorstep gold loans, business and micro enterprise loans, micro personal loans, micro home loans, micro finance and consumer goods loans. Gold loans are approved within 15–30 minutes of valuation and verification.

The doorstep gold loan brings valuation to the customer's home. A field executive checks and seals the gold, and the customer signs at the Thaikad branch, as the KML Act requires. Teknoppy was brought in to design the website and make these services easy to find on Google.

06 · Our approach

Our Approach

Step 01

Local search research first

We studied how people in Guruvayoor search for gold loans, doorstep gold service, micro finance and registered lenders before planning any page.

Step 02

One page per loan type

Gold loan, gold loan in Guruvayoor, doorstep gold loan, business loan, micro enterprise, micro personal, micro home, micro finance and consumer goods loans each have their own page and target search.

Step 03

KML trust signals on every page

KML registration is stated in the home page title and content, with a dedicated KML Registration and Compliance page plus grievance redressal, customer rights, privacy and terms pages in the footer.

Step 04

On-page optimisation

Titles, H1s and meta descriptions pair each service with Guruvayoor, such as "KML-Registered Loan Company in Guruvayoor, Kerala" on the home page.

Step 05

Local SEO and schema

We kept the business name, Thaikad address and phone number consistent across the website and Google Business Profile, and added financial service and FAQ schema.

Step 06

Answer-first, compliant content

Pages answer what borrowers ask: how fast approval is, how doorstep valuation works, why the branch visit is required and how to repay, without promising rates.

10 · Why trust signals

Why Trust Signals Decide Which Lender Ranks in Guruvayoor

Someone pledging family gold for a loan is making a money decision that affects their household. Before they call, they want to know the lender is registered, where the branch is and how the process works. Google treats finance as a "Your Money or Your Life" topic and favours pages that show exactly that.

Abrus Krishna Finance already had what borrowers look for: KML registration, a local branch in Thaikad and a clear, fast process. Our work was to put that evidence on every page and give each loan its own search target. It is the same structured approach we used in our Greens Ayurveda SEO case study.

Key takeaway: In finance search, the lender that proves it can be trusted is the lender that gets found.

Is Abrus Krishna Finance KML-registered?

What loans does Abrus Krishna Finance offer?

Gold loans, doorstep gold loans, business loans, micro enterprise loans, micro personal loans, micro home loans, micro finance and consumer goods loans.

How does the doorstep gold loan work?

A field executive values the gold at the customer's home and seals it for the branch vault. The customer then signs the agreement at the branch, as the KML Act requires, and the loan is paid out.
