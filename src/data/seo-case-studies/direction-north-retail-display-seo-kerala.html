02 · Background

About Direction North

Brand managers and trade marketing teams need floor standing units, countertop displays, standees and exhibition stalls, often against tight launch dates. They search Google for a maker who can design, build and install across Kerala.

Direction North designs, manufactures and installs retail displays and branding from Kochi. Its range runs from flanges, wobblers and endcap branding to floor standing units, countertop units, kiosks, exhibition stalls, corporate gift kits, custom awards and bulk return gifts. Everything is made in-house, so one vendor handles design, production and installation.

03 · The challenge

Many products, two audiences and a statewide market

Three problems shaped the SEO strategy.

01

Many products, each with its own search terms

Buyers search for "FSU display", "countertop display unit", "standee printing" or "exhibition stall". A single services page cannot rank for all of these.

02

B2B buyers across the whole state

Clients in Kozhikode or Thiruvananthapuram will not find a Kochi maker unless Google sees clear signals that it delivers and installs there.

03

Two very different audiences

Retail display buyers are brand teams. Gift buyers are HR teams and families planning weddings. Each group needs its own keywords and pages.

06 · Our approach

How Teknoppy did the SEO

5 connected pillars, each answering a problem from the audit.

Pillar 01 · Research

Keyword research and mapping

Each group of searches has one target page, so pages never compete.

Pillar 02 · On-page

On-page and technical SEO

The title reads "Retail Display & Visual Merchandising Manufacturer in Kerala". Robots allow full indexing with large image previews, the canonical tag is set, Open Graph and Twitter cards are in place, images are served as WebP, and Google Search Console is verified.

Pillar 03 · Content

FAQ content for answer engines

An 11-question FAQ answers what buyers ask before a quote, including who the best retail display manufacturer in Kerala is, turnaround times and delivery across the state. These answers can win featured snippets and AI answers.

Pillar 04 · Local

Local SEO across Kerala

The full Kochi address, phone, WhatsApp, hours and GST number sit in the footer. Copy and project references name Kozhikode, Thrissur, Kollam, Kannur and Thiruvananthapuram, and trust badges confirm pan-Kerala delivery.

Pillar 05 · Trust

Industry and trust signals

An industries grid targets FMCG, pharma, telecom, QSR, modern trade and automotive buyers. Client logos, a 5-star Google rating badge and a "Why choose us" section support trust, and every section leads to "Request a Quote".

11 · Direct answers

Frequently asked questions

How long does SEO take for a manufacturer in Kerala?

Most B2B sites see first ranking movement in 3 to 6 months. Product and city searches with less competition usually move first.

Should each display product have its own page?

Yes. Separate pages for FSUs, countertop units, standees and stalls match how buyers search and let each page rank.

Can a Kochi company rank in other Kerala cities?

Yes. Naming the cities you serve, showing projects there and keeping business details consistent helps Google show you across the state.

Do FAQs help B2B SEO?

Yes. FAQs answer real buyer questions, can win featured snippets and give AI tools clear answers to quote.

What does an SEO audit include?

Titles, headings, alt text, internal links, indexing, speed, local details and content gaps, with a clear action list.
