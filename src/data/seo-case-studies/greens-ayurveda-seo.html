03 · About

About Greens Ayurveda

Greens Ayurveda is an Ayurveda hospital, Panchakarma centre and training institute in Azhiyur, a coastal village in Kozhikode district. It is classified "Ayur Gold" by Kerala Tourism and welcomes guests from Europe, Russia, the Americas and the Gulf.

The centre is led by founder and Chief Physician Dr. C. P. Asghar, BAMS (Kottakkal), PGDYT (KMC Manipal). The campus has 62 guest rooms and more than 50 staff, and classical medicines are prepared on site.

Greens also runs practical courses of 10 days to 4 weeks in Panchakarma, massage, nutrition, yoga, Marma and beauty therapy. Teknoppy was brought in to make that experience visible in Google search.

04 · The challenge

The Challenge

01

Real expertise, weak search signals

Search engines could not connect the doctors' qualifications and decades of practice to the website.

02

Headings and meta tags that said too little

Key pages used generic headings and overlong meta descriptions that did not tell Google what each page was about.

03

Treatments and courses competing for the same searches

Panchakarma treatment and Panchakarma training targeted overlapping keywords, splitting rankings between pages.

04

A brand known by several names

"Greens Ayurveda", "Greens Ayur Center" and "ayugreen" appeared across listings, weakening the business in search.

05

International patients research before they book

Overseas guests ask about doctors, duration, food and safety. Most pages did not answer them directly.

Key takeaway: Experience that search engines cannot see does not bring in patients.

05 · Project objective

Project Objective

The goal was to make Greens Ayurveda the most trusted search result for Ayurveda treatment and training in Kerala. We set four objectives:

  1. 01Prove medical expertise with clear doctor signals
  2. 02Give every important search one clear page to rank
  3. 03Unify the brand into one business across the web
  4. 04Answer patient and student questions in plain language
06 · Our approach

Our Approach

Step 01

Search-intent research first

We studied how patients and students actually search, by treatment, course, condition and location, before changing any page.

Step 02

One search intent per page

We mapped every keyword to one page. "Panchakarma treatment in Kerala" belongs to the treatment page, and "Panchakarma course in Kerala" belongs to the course page.

Step 03

Doctor-backed E-E-A-T

Clinical pages now show who supervises the treatment, with Dr. C. P. Asghar's qualifications and role, and are reviewed by him.

Step 04

On-page optimisation

We rewrote titles, H1s and meta descriptions around each page's target search, and cleaned up the heading structure.

Step 05

One name, one address, one set of details

We aligned the business name, address and phone across Google Business Profile, Kerala Tourism, TripAdvisor and directories, and added MedicalClinic, Physician, Course and FAQPage schema.

Step 06

Answer-first content

Key pages open with a short, direct answer: what the treatment is, who it suits, how long it takes and who supervises it.

10 · Why E-E-A-T

Why E-E-A-T Decides Which Ayurveda Centre Patients Trust

A guest planning a 21-day Panchakarma programme is making a health decision and a travel decision at once. They want to know who will treat them and how qualified that person is. Google judges health content the same way, favouring pages that show real experience and expertise.

Greens already had this: a qualified Chief Physician, a resident medical team and loyal returning guests. Our work was to show that evidence clearly, without medical promises, and to give each search one page. We used the same doctor-backed approach in our Ayurvedic spine hospital SEO case study.

Key takeaway: In health search, being trusted and being found are the same job.

11 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Greens Ayurveda located?

Greens Ayurveda is in Azhiyur, Kozhikode (Calicut) district, Kerala, India, near Mahe.

Who leads the medical team at Greens Ayurveda?

Dr. C. P. Asghar, BAMS (Kottakkal), PGDYT (KMC Manipal), the founder and Chief Physician.

What treatments and courses does Greens Ayurveda offer?

Panchakarma, Kerala massages, Shirodhara, Marma and yoga therapy, plus courses of 10 days to 4 weeks.

What services did Teknoppy provide for Greens Ayurveda?

SEO strategy, keyword mapping, E-E-A-T and entity structure, on-page optimisation, local SEO and schema markup.

Did Teknoppy redesign the Greens Ayurveda website?

No. This was an SEO project on the existing WordPress website, ayugreen.com.

How did Teknoppy avoid keyword cannibalisation?

We mapped every keyword to one page and gave treatments and courses separate search targets.

Why does E-E-A-T matter for Ayurveda websites?

Health is a "Your Money or Your Life" topic, so Google favours pages that show who the doctors are and what qualifies them.

Does Teknoppy do SEO for other Ayurveda centres in Kerala?

Yes. We work with Ayurveda hospitals, resorts and wellness brands across Kerala, the UAE and Qatar.
