02 · Background

About Mount Bell

Choosing an immigration consultant is a big, costly and personal decision. Nurses, tradespeople and IT professionals in Kerala search carefully, compare several firms and look hard for proof that a consultant is licensed and honest.

Mount Bell helps Kerala professionals move abroad for work and permanent residence, with a focus on healthcare, skilled trades, IT and teaching. It runs in-house language and test training, offers a free first consultation in Malayalam and English, shares fees in writing before payment, and states clearly that it does not guarantee jobs or visas.

03 · The challenge

A high-trust decision in a crowded market

Three problems shaped the SEO strategy.

01

A crowded, high-trust market

Kerala has hundreds of visa and immigration agents. Google treats immigration as "Your Money or Your Life" content, so it favours sites that clearly prove experience, expertise and trustworthiness.

02

Searches split by country, job and test

People search "Australia PR for nurses", "Germany Opportunity Card", "OET coaching Thrissur" or "Denmark work visa". One general page cannot rank for all of them.

03

Clients come from every district

With offices only in Kochi and Thrissur, Mount Bell needed to reach applicants searching in Calicut, Kannur, Kottayam, Trivandrum and beyond.

06 · Our approach

How Teknoppy did the SEO

6 connected pillars, each answering a problem from the audit.

Pillar 01 · Research

Keyword research and mapping

Each search intent has its own target page, so pages never compete.

Pillar 02 · On-page

On-page SEO

The title reads "Immigration Consultancy in Kerala – PR & Visas | Mount Bell" and the H1 is "Immigration Consultancy in Kerala for PR, Work Visas and Jobs Abroad". H2s cover destinations, nursing, services, training, calculators, process, licence, offices and FAQ. Open Graph tags and Search Console are set up.

Pillar 03 · Trust

E-E-A-T trust content

For immigration advice, trust is the ranking factor that matters most. The MEA licence number appears in several places with an eMigrate verification link, fees are published, a disclaimer says outcomes are not guaranteed, a named team page is linked, and testimonials and office photos show real people.

Pillar 04 · Tools

Free tools that earn visits and links

Australia PR points, Germany Opportunity Card points and a general eligibility checker answer the first question every applicant has. Tools like these attract repeat visits, shares and natural links.

Pillar 05 · Local

Local and district SEO

Kochi and Thrissur addresses, maps and phone numbers support local rankings, while nine district pages cover Calicut, Kannur, Kollam, Kottayam, Malappuram, Palakkad, Trivandrum and both office cities.

Pillar 06 · Content

FAQ and blog for answer engines

An 8-question FAQ and a blog with regular updates answer real applicant questions, helping Mount Bell win featured snippets and be quoted in AI answers.

11 · Direct answers

Frequently asked questions

Why is trust so important for immigration SEO?

Google treats immigration as high-stakes advice. Sites that show licences, real people, clear fees and honest disclaimers are more likely to rank.

Do district pages help a consultancy rank across Kerala?

Yes, when each page is useful and specific. They help Google show the firm to people searching in districts without an office.

Do free calculators help SEO?

Yes. Points and eligibility calculators answer a common question, bring repeat visitors and earn natural links.

How long does SEO take for an immigration consultant?

Most see first ranking movement in 3 to 6 months. Country and test searches with less competition usually move first.

Should a consultancy write blog posts about visa rule changes?

Yes. Timely updates answer urgent questions, keep the site fresh and show expertise.
