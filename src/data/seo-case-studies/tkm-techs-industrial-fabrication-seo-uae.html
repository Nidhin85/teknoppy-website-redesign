02 · Background

About TKM Techs UAE

TKM Technical Services designs, fabricates, installs and maintains stainless steel tanks, hygienic piping, conveyors and turnkey process lines from its own Sharjah workshop. Its buyers are plant engineers and project managers in food, dairy, beverage, pharma, chemical and oil and gas. They search in technical terms and want proof first.

TKM is a one-team fabrication company. Its engineers, welders and site technicians handle design, TIG welding, passivation and polishing, installation, PLC and HMI programming, and maintenance. It works in SS304, SS316L, duplex, mild steel and carbon steel, builds tanks up to 1,800 m³, and offers emergency response to UAE sites.

03 · The challenge

Technical buyers, many products and a regional market

Three problems shaped the SEO strategy.

01

A wide range of products, each with its own searches

Food grade tanks, jacketed mixing tanks, silos, CIP systems and conveyors are all different searches. One general page cannot rank for all of them.

02

Technical buyers who need proof

Dairy and pharma engineers check hygienic design, material grades and certificates before shortlisting. Thin pages lose them.

03

Local and regional reach

TKM is in Sharjah but serves Abu Dhabi, Saudi Arabia and Oman. Google needs clear location signals.

06 · Our approach

How Teknoppy did the SEO

6 connected pillars, each answering a problem from the audit.

Pillar 01 · Research

Keyword research and mapping

Each layer of searches gets its own target page, so pages never compete.

Pillar 02 · On-page

On-page SEO

The homepage leads with "Industrial Fabrication Company in UAE, Built in Sharjah", with clear H2 and H3 headings for tanks, piping, fabrication, process equipment and maintenance.

Pillar 03 · Trust

E-E-A-T trust content

We put the proof on the page: ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 with a register link, mill test certificates, factory acceptance testing, workshop size, team numbers, a named project director, a 12-month warranty and a testimonial.

11 · Direct answers

Frequently asked questions

How long does SEO take for a manufacturing company in the UAE?

Most B2B sites see first ranking movement in 3 to 6 months. Long-tail product searches usually move first.

What keywords should a fabrication company target?

A mix of broad terms such as "industrial fabrication company UAE", product terms such as "food grade tanks UAE", industry terms such as "dairy processing tanks", and location terms such as "fabrication company Sharjah".

Why does E-E-A-T matter for industrial websites?

Buyers and Google both look for proof. Certificates, material grades, test documents and real projects make a page more trustworthy.

Can a Sharjah company rank in Abu Dhabi, Saudi Arabia or Oman?

Yes. Service-area content and location pages help Google show a company in the areas it serves.

What does an SEO audit include?

Meta titles, headings, alt text, internal links, speed, indexing, local details and content gaps.
