02 · Background

About Creative Web Design Qatar

Creative Web Design Qatar is a web design and digital services agency founded in 2005 and based in Doha Tower on Al Asmak Street, Doha. It builds websites, eCommerce stores and mobile apps, and offers SEO, web hosting, domain and email hosting, logo design, social media marketing, Google Ads management, content writing, website maintenance, and Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace setup, for clients across corporate, retail, healthcare, real estate, education and hospitality sectors.

With an exact-match domain and two decades in the market, the agency already ranked for its core terms. The problem was depth. The homepage carried most of the visibility, inner service pages had little content of their own to rank with, and the structured data across the site contained errors and conflicting details that weakened the signals search engines and AI tools rely on.

This case study covers the schema markup, content strategy and blog content delivered by Teknoppy. It does not cover the agency's own design, development or hosting work for its clients, which is the company's own domain.

03 · The challenge

Helping an established agency rank beyond its homepage

Our first audit found five problems holding the site back.

01

The homepage was doing all the work

The site appeared on page one for "web design company Qatar" and "website design Doha Qatar", but service pages such as SEO, hosting and logo design were not ranking for their own head terms.

02

Structured data with errors and duplicates

Existing schema pointed to the wrong YouTube channel and declared the business twice, as both LocalBusiness and ProfessionalService, sending search engines two competing versions of the same entity.

03

Conflicting facts across pages

The homepage said the agency was founded in 2005 while service pages said 2001. Inconsistent facts like this weaken trust signals for both Google and AI answer engines.

04

No content to support service pages

There was no planned blog content feeding the service pages, so pages like SEO, hosting and eCommerce had nothing building topical authority or internal links around them.

05

A crowded, competitive market

Qatar web design and SEO searches are dominated by directories such as Clutch and many established agencies. Ranking higher meant out-answering competitors on the questions buyers actually ask, not just repeating service lists.

04 · Project objective

What Teknoppy was engaged to do

Teknoppy was engaged to strengthen the site's technical trust signals and build a content engine that helps every service page rank on its own. The brief centred on:

  1. 01Checking where the site actually ranked to find the real gaps
  2. 02Building verified, connected schema markup for the homepage and every service page
  3. 03Fixing errors, duplicates and conflicting facts in the existing structured data
  4. 04Planning a pillar-based blog programme that supports each service page
  5. 05Writing SEO, AEO, GEO and E-E-A-T optimized blog content for priority topics
05 · Our approach

How we built verified schema and a pillar-based content engine

Six pillars took the site from homepage-only rankings to a structure where every service can compete.

Pillar 01 · Research

A ranking check before any recommendation

Teknoppy tested the site's core terms first. It appeared mid-page on page one for "web design company Qatar" and "website design Doha Qatar", behind directories and established competitors, and was not visible for "SEO company Qatar Doha". That showed the strength sat with the homepage and the exact-match domain, while inner pages needed their own support.

Pillar 02 · Verified data

Every fact checked against the live site

Before writing any markup, Teknoppy confirmed the company name, address, both phone numbers, WhatsApp, email, founding year, social profiles and services directly from the live pages. Nothing went into the schema that the site itself did not state.

Pillar 03 · Schema graph

One connected entity across the whole site

Three global blocks — Organization, ProfessionalService and WebSite — go on every page, linked by shared @id references. Each service page then adds its own Service and breadcrumb block, so search engines read the site as one company with a clear, connected list of services.

Pillar 04 · Fixes

Six structured data issues resolved

The wrong YouTube channel was replaced with the verified one. Duplicate LocalBusiness and ProfessionalService entities were merged into one. A site-search action that a static HTML site cannot support was removed. The 2005 versus 2001 founding-year conflict and the opening hours were flagged for the client to confirm, and FAQ markup was updated for Google's current rules.

Pillar 05 · Content plan

60+ blog topics across nine service pillars

Blog topics were grouped into nine pillars that mirror the agency's services: website design, website development, eCommerce, SEO and AEO, mobile apps, digital marketing, hosting and maintenance, industry and location content, and branding and productivity. Each post has one keyword and one service page it supports, and high-priority topics such as website cost in Qatar were flagged to publish first.

Pillar 06 · Writing

Answer-first blog content

Posts are written to open with the primary keyword and answer the question directly, use secondary keywords naturally without stuffing, and carry a meta title, meta description and permalink within character limits. The first, a comparison of cloud and traditional hosting for Qatar businesses, was kept under 1,300 words and links back to the hosting service page.

09 · Strategic reasoning

Why this approach matters

An established site with an exact-match domain can rank its homepage for years while its service pages stay invisible. That leaves real revenue on the table, because buyers searching for SEO, hosting or eCommerce in Qatar land on competitors' dedicated pages instead. Building content pillars that feed each service page, and linking them carefully, lets authority spread from the homepage to the pages that actually match what buyers search for.

Clean structured data matters even more now that AI tools answer so many supplier questions. When ChatGPT, Gemini or Google AI Overviews look up a web design company in Doha, they lean on consistent facts — one entity, one founding year, one address, verified profiles — and on content that answers questions like how much a website costs in Qatar directly. Duplicate entities and conflicting details make an agency harder to recommend with confidence.

Giving search engines one verified, connected identity for Creative Web Design Qatar — and giving every service page its own pillar of answer-first content — is what turns a homepage that ranks into a whole site that competes.

10 · Due diligence FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What was the main SEO problem for Creative Web Design Qatar?

The homepage was doing almost all of the ranking work. It appeared on page one for core web design terms in Qatar, but inner service pages such as SEO, hosting and logo design were not ranking for their own head terms, and the structured data had conflicts and errors.

What schema markup did Teknoppy build?

Teknoppy built a connected set of Organization, ProfessionalService, WebSite, WebPage, Service and BreadcrumbList blocks. Global blocks go on every page, and each service page gets its own Service and breadcrumb block, all linked by shared @id references and verified against the live pages.

Which schema issues were fixed?

Teknoppy corrected a wrong YouTube channel link, merged duplicate LocalBusiness and ProfessionalService entities into one, removed a SearchAction that a static HTML site cannot support, flagged a 2005 versus 2001 founding-year conflict, flagged opening hours for verification, and updated FAQ markup handling for current Google rules.

How was the blog content plan structured?

The plan covers 60+ blog topics grouped into nine pillars that match the agency's services, from website design and development to SEO, hosting, mobile apps and branding. Every topic has a target keyword, search intent, funnel stage, format, an AEO question, a location, an E-E-A-T angle, the service page it supports, and a priority.

Why does each blog post link to a service page?

Blogs target informational and comparison searches, then pass relevance and internal links to the commercial service page they support. This builds topical authority around each service without the blog competing with the service page for the same buying keyword.

Where did Creative Web Design Qatar rank before the content push?

A directional search check in June 2026 found the site on page one for "web design company Qatar" and "website design Doha Qatar", mid-page in a crowded field. It was not visible for "SEO company Qatar Doha", which is one of the gaps the content plan targets.

How does this help the agency appear in AI search tools?

Clean, connected schema tells search engines and AI tools exactly who the company is, where it is and what it offers, while answer-first blog posts give ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews direct passages to quote for questions like how much a website costs in Qatar.

Does this approach work for other agencies and service businesses?

Yes. Any established business whose homepage carries most of its rankings benefits from verified schema on every page and a pillar-based content plan that builds authority for each service page individually.
