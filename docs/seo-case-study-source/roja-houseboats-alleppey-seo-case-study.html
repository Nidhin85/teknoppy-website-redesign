02 · Background

About Roja Houseboats

Roja Houseboats is a houseboat operator on the Alappuzha backwaters, running since 2014 from its boarding point at Mannancherry, near Alleppey Lighthouse and a few minutes from Alappuzha railway station. It offers 1, 2 and 3 bedroom houseboats with day cruises, overnight stays, multi-day tours and special-event charters, with packages starting from ₹6,000 per couple for a day cruise. The business states Kerala Tourism Department certification and a 4.9 out of 5 guest rating.

The website, built on WordPress with Elementor, already had the right pages — a homepage and separate 1, 2 and 3 bedroom houseboat pages — but small on-page issues were holding it back. The H1 repeated the title tag, the page showed two different phone numbers, social icons linked to platform homepages, and the FAQs did not answer travellers' questions in the first sentence. Nothing told Google, or a cautious first-time visitor, who actually runs the boats.

This case study covers the SEO audit, on-page content and local SEO work delivered by Teknoppy. It does not cover Roja Houseboats' cruise operations, crew or pricing decisions, which are the company's own domain.

03 · The challenge

Fixing the small on-page issues that cost a tourism site bookings

Our audit found five problems holding the site back.

01

A weak, duplicated H1

The homepage H1 was the full title tag repeated word for word, with no clear value statement, price signal or trust number for a traveller to see first.

02

Inconsistent contact details

Two different phone numbers appeared on the same page, one of them a placeholder. For a local business, mismatched contact details weaken Google Maps trust and can send a booking to a dead line.

03

FAQs that did not answer first

Answers opened with background rather than a direct answer, and the two most-searched questions in the category — houseboat cost and best operator in Alleppey — were missing entirely.

04

No visible people behind the business

There was no About section, founder story or licence detail, and social icons linked to Facebook and YouTube homepages rather than Roja's own profiles — leaving almost no E-E-A-T signal.

05

Outdated keyword density advice

The content had been planned around 2.5% keyword density, which reads as keyword stuffing today. The pages needed natural keyword use supported by related backwater and Kerala terms instead.

05 · Our approach

How we optimized a houseboat website for local search and answer engines

Six pillars took the site from good pages with small leaks to a consistent, trustworthy booking site.

Pillar 01 · Audit

Section-by-section homepage audit

Teknoppy reviewed every homepage section from the hero to the footer and wrote exact instructions for each — what to keep, what to edit and what to add — so the client's team could make each change in Elementor without interpreting general SEO advice.

Pillar 02 · Keywords

One keyword per houseboat size

The homepage owns "houseboat booking in Alleppey", while the 1, 2 and 3 bedroom pages each own their room-type keyword. Teknoppy set a natural density of roughly 1–1.5%, supported by related terms like Kerala houseboat, Alleppey backwaters and backwater cruise, replacing the earlier 2.5% target.

Pillar 03 · AEO

Answer-first FAQs

Every FAQ answer now opens with a direct, standalone sentence — for example, that the best time for an Alleppey houseboat trip is October to February. Two new questions on houseboat cost in Alleppey and the best houseboat operator were added, written around the package prices and rating the site already states.

Pillar 04 · Local SEO

Consistent NAP and a mapped boarding point

One phone number was set for every button and section, the placeholder number was flagged for removal, and GPS coordinates, parking details and a Google Maps embed of the Mannancherry boarding point were added to the contact section, with transport times from Cochin Airport and Alappuzha station.

Pillar 05 · E-E-A-T

Real people and real credentials

A new About Roja Houseboats section tells the founding story from Mannancherry, with a recommended founder name and photo. The footer gains Kerala Tourism licence details, privacy, terms and cancellation policy links, and social icons pointing to Roja's actual profiles.

Pillar 06 · Content

Room-type page copy in two languages

Teknoppy wrote keyword-focused paragraphs for the 3 bedroom houseboat Alleppey page — capacity for 6 to 8 guests, three AC bedrooms with attached bathrooms, sun deck and onboard chef — in several lengths, plus a Malayalam version for local travellers, each using the keyword naturally.

09 · Strategic reasoning

Why these fixes matter

For a local tourism business, small inconsistencies cost real bookings. A wrong phone number on a Call Now button, a social icon that leads nowhere, or a Maps pin Google is unsure about each make a traveller hesitate — and a hesitating traveller books the next houseboat in the results. Making contact details consistent, mapping the boarding point and showing who runs the boats removes those doubts for both guests and Google.

Answer-first FAQs matter because travellers increasingly plan trips by asking. When someone asks Google AI Overviews or ChatGPT how much a houseboat costs in Alleppey or when to go, those tools quote the page that answers in its first sentence. Giving each room type its own page and each common question a direct answer gives Roja Houseboats more chances to be that quoted answer.

One verified phone number, one keyword per boat, and one direct answer per question — that consistency is what lets search engines and AI assistants trust Roja Houseboats as a clear, bookable answer for travellers planning an Alleppey backwater trip.

10 · Due diligence FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What were the main SEO problems on the Roja Houseboats website?

The homepage H1 simply repeated the title tag, two different phone numbers appeared on the page, social links pointed to platform homepages instead of Roja's own profiles, FAQ answers did not open with a direct answer, and there was no founder or About section to show who runs the business.

How did Teknoppy organise the houseboat pages?

Each houseboat size has its own page and keyword: 1 bedroom, 2 bedroom and 3 bedroom houseboat Alleppey. The homepage targets houseboat booking in Alleppey, so no two pages compete for the same search.

What keyword density does Teknoppy recommend?

Teknoppy recommends roughly 1 to 1.5% for a primary keyword, supported by related terms such as Kerala houseboat, Alleppey backwaters and backwater cruise. Density of 2.5% or more reads as keyword stuffing and can hurt rankings.

How were the FAQs improved for answer engines?

Every FAQ answer was rewritten to open with a direct, standalone sentence, and two high-intent questions were added: how much a houseboat costs in Alleppey, and which houseboat operator is best in Alleppey. These target featured snippets, People Also Ask and Google AI Overviews.

Why does NAP consistency matter for a houseboat business?

Name, address and phone details need to match everywhere so Google can trust which business and boarding point it is showing on Maps. Teknoppy recommended one phone number across the site, GPS coordinates in the contact section, and a Google Maps embed of the boarding point.

What E-E-A-T improvements were made?

Teknoppy added an About Roja Houseboats section with the founding story from Mannancherry, recommended a founder name and photo, Kerala Tourism licence details in the footer, and privacy, terms and cancellation policy pages.

Was content written for the room-type pages?

Yes. Teknoppy wrote keyword-focused paragraphs for the 3 bedroom houseboat Alleppey page in English and Malayalam, covering capacity, bedrooms, onboard chef, sun deck and booking, with the keyword used naturally.

Does this approach work for other Kerala tourism businesses?

Yes. Any houseboat, resort or tour operator benefits from one keyword per page, answer-first FAQs, consistent contact details, and clear signals about who runs the business.
