02 · Background

About General Tech Services

General Tech Services LLC is a calibration laboratory and industrial equipment supplier founded in 1998 by Mr. John Mathew and headquartered in Industrial Area 11, Sharjah, with an office in Abu Dhabi and sister companies in Dammam, Saudi Arabia and East Ahmadi, Kuwait. Its laboratory is ISO/IEC 17025 accredited through EIAC (LB-CAL-004) and ENAS (NAL 240), covering 15 calibration disciplines from dimensional, electrical and pressure to biomedical, NDT and gas detection, carried out on-site or in-lab.

The company runs three businesses under one name: accredited calibration, authorized distribution of more than 50 global brands including Mitutoyo, WIKA, Norbar, Snap-on and Control Techniques, and engineering services such as VFD repair, CMM inspection, PRV testing and live panel cleaning. It reports more than 33,000 calibrations a year, a team of 60+ engineers, and a 10,000 sq ft Sharjah facility.

That breadth created the SEO problem. "General Tech" is a generic name shared by unrelated companies, and one website had to explain three different businesses to search engines at once. This case study covers the keyword strategy, answer-engine content and product page SEO delivered by Teknoppy. It does not cover General Tech's calibration methods, laboratory procedures or accreditation process, which are the company's own domain.

03 · The challenge

Giving a generic brand name a clear, searchable identity

Our first audit found five problems holding the site back.

01

A generic brand name with no qualifier

"General Tech" returns unrelated IT firms and job listings. Without accreditation and location words attached to the brand, search engines and AI tools could not reliably tell which company a searcher meant.

02

Three businesses competing for one homepage

Calibration, product supply and engineering services were all pulling the homepage in different directions, so no single business line sent a strong, focused relevance signal.

03

Buyer questions went unanswered

Procurement and quality teams ask direct questions: is the lab accredited, is on-site calibration available, what does it cost. The site had no structured answers that a featured snippet or AI assistant could lift.

04

Thin, manufacturer-copy product pages

Store pages for instruments, drives and transmitters largely repeated manufacturer text, gave buyers no reason to choose General Tech, and risked competing with the store's own category pages for broad terms.

05

Trust signals buried instead of stated

The strongest proof points — EIAC and ENAS accreditation numbers, 15 disciplines, authorized-distributor status, 28+ years in business — were not written into the content in a way search engines could read as E-E-A-T evidence.

04 · Project objective

What Teknoppy was engaged to do

Teknoppy was engaged to build an SEO, AEO, GEO and E-E-A-T strategy that makes General Tech Services the clear answer for accredited calibration and industrial supply searches in the UAE. The brief centred on:

  1. 01Defining one primary keyword that ties the brand to its accreditation, services and location
  2. 02Building a supporting keyword set across brand, service, location, question and trust intent
  3. 03Writing an answer-first FAQ hub that serves featured snippets, voice search and AI answer engines
  4. 04Turning accreditation, scope and brand partnerships into readable E-E-A-T content on the homepage

05 · Our approach

How we built an SEO, AEO and GEO strategy around one clear entity

Six pillars took the site from a generic name to one well-defined search identity.

Pillar 01 · Primary keyword

Anchoring the brand to accreditation and location

Teknoppy set the primary keyword as "General Tech Services — ISO 17025 Calibration Laboratory and Industrial Equipment Supplier UAE". It joins the brand name to its accreditation, its two core business lines and its country, so the brand stays the identity anchor while every qualifier search engines need is attached to it. The phrase leads the homepage title, the first paragraph and the closing contact block.

Pillar 02 · Keyword architecture

A 50-keyword set in five intent clusters

Supporting keywords were grouped into five clusters of ten: brand core terms (General Tech Services Sharjah, EIAC accredited), calibration service terms (on-site calibration services UAE), GEO location terms (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, GCC), AEO question terms (where to buy Mitutoyo equipment in UAE), and E-E-A-T trust terms (authorized Norbar distributor UAE).

Pillar 03 · E-E-A-T

Writing proof points into the homepage

An eight-point trust section now states the evidence directly: EIAC and ENAS accreditation, operation since 1998, authorized distribution for Mitutoyo, WIKA and Norbar, on-site calibration across all seven Emirates with a 20+ vehicle fleet, the sectors served, and every one of the 15 accredited disciplines named in full.

Pillar 04 · AEO

A 25-question answer-engine FAQ hub

The FAQ page covers 25 buyer questions in seven sections — company, calibration services, ISO 17025 and EIAC, calibration types, certificates and traceability, repairs and rental, and location. Every answer opens by restating the question so it can be lifted as a standalone snippet, and the page carries FAQPage schema for rich results.

Pillar 05 · Product SEO

Store product pages rebuilt from verified specs

Product pages on generaltechshop.ae were rewritten with a fixed structure, each targeting a model-number keyword rather than a broad category term, so product pages never compete with the collection pages that own those terms. Specifications came only from the live product page or manufacturer datasheets, and discrepancies — such as a title that contradicted its model code — were flagged for the client instead of guessed.

Pillar 06 · Service & local

Service keyword sets and regional profiles

Individual service pages, such as live panel cleaning, received their own keyword sets covering technical terms, compliance standards and trust signals, keeping each service distinct from the others. Teknoppy also audited the Saudi Arabia Google Business Profile against Google's guidelines and local Saudi requirements to support the company's GCC presence.

09 · Strategic reasoning

Why this strategy matters

A generic brand name is one of the hardest problems in B2B SEO. When the name alone could mean dozens of companies, search engines lean on the words around it to decide who you are. Attaching accreditation, service lines and location to the brand in a consistent primary keyword gives every page the same clear signal, so authority builds on one entity instead of spreading thin across an ambiguous name.

The same approach is what AI-driven search needs. When a procurement manager asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google AI Overviews for an accredited calibration lab in Sharjah, those tools look for content that names the company, its accreditation numbers, its disciplines, its brands and its locations explicitly — and answers the question directly. An answer-first FAQ hub and entity-led trust content give them exactly that, which matters as more UAE businesses start supplier research by asking an AI tool.

Anchoring a generic name to its accreditation, its services and its location — then answering every buyer question directly — is what lets search engines and AI tools resolve General Tech Services as one clearly defined calibration and supply partner, rather than one of many companies with a similar name.

10 · Due diligence FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What was the main SEO problem with General Tech Services before this project?

The brand name "General Tech" is generic and shared by unrelated companies, and one website was trying to represent three different businesses: an accredited calibration lab, an authorized product distributor, and an engineering services team. Search engines had no single, clear signal for what General Tech Services actually is.

What primary keyword did Teknoppy choose for General Tech Services?

The primary keyword is "General Tech Services — ISO 17025 Calibration Laboratory and Industrial Equipment Supplier UAE". It ties the brand name to its accreditation, its two core business lines, and its country, so search engines and AI tools can tell it apart from similarly named companies.

How many keywords were mapped in the strategy?

Teknoppy built a 50-keyword set in five clusters of ten: brand core keywords, calibration service keywords, GEO location keywords, AEO question-based keywords, and E-E-A-T trust keywords.

What is AEO and how was it used for General Tech Services?

AEO, or answer engine optimization, means writing content that directly answers the questions buyers ask. Teknoppy built a 25-question FAQ hub across seven topic sections, where every answer opens by restating the question, and the page is marked up with FAQPage schema.

How were the generaltechshop.ae product pages optimized?

Each product page was rewritten from verified source data using a fixed structure: SEO title, meta title, short summary with a call-to-action, benefits, a specification table, buyer FAQ and meta description. Every page targets its own model-number keyword so product pages do not compete with category pages.

How does the strategy help General Tech Services appear in AI search tools?

The content names the company, its accreditation numbers (EIAC LB-CAL-004 and ENAS NAL 240), its 15 calibration disciplines, its brands and its locations explicitly. That entity-based language is what tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews use to summarize and recommend a supplier.

Is the new content live?

Yes. The homepage keyword strategy, trust content and FAQ content are live on generaltech.ae, and product page rewrites are being rolled out across the generaltechshop.ae store.

Does this approach work for other calibration labs or industrial distributors?

Yes. Any B2B business with a generic name, several service lines and a large product catalogue benefits from anchoring the brand to its accreditation and location, giving each service and product its own keyword, and answering buyer questions directly.
